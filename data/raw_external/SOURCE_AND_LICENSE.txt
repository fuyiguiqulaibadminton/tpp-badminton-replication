Note (per depositors): the Elo ratings are model-based measures constructed by
the original authors and are NOT official BWF statistics. These files are
redistributed here for review reproducibility under CC BY 4.0 attribution;
they are also freely downloadable from the Dataset DOI above.

Only the three data tables are redistributed. The original release scripts
(01_load_data.py ... 05_verify_elo.py) are available at the Dataset DOI.

This study uses only the pre-match Elo columns (winner_elo, loser_elo),
match_date, tournament_id, type, round, winner_id/loser_id and score, linked
to players.xlsx names. Coverage: MS/WS only, 2006-09 to 2025-12; no doubles
Elo exists. The 2026 World Championships therefore cannot be matched.
